# Flow Reader

Read PDFs, books (EPUB), Word documents, text files and videos on your phone — highlight, underline, comment, keep time notes on videos, define words in English, Malayalam and Arabic, and export a summary PDF of your marks.

**Use it on iPhone or iPad:** open the link below in Safari, tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

https://raziihsan1-ops.github.io/flow-reader/

Everything you add stays on your own device. Dictionary: Wordset/WordNet (CC BY-SA 4.0), Olam (ODbL), Wiktionary via kaikki.org (CC BY-SA).
